How Can You Delete Your Data?

All of your data in the I Ching: Wu Xing 五行 application is stored only on your device. You can use any of the methods below to delete your data:

1
Delete consultation history from within the app (Recommended)
Open the I Ching app → Tap the clock icon (history) → In the panel that opens, press the "Clear History" button → Confirm on the confirmation screen. All of your hexagram consultation records are deleted immediately and permanently.
2
Reset application data
Follow this path on Android: Settings → Apps → I Ching → Clear Storage. All application data — including consultation history, language preference, and all settings — is deleted.
3
Uninstall the application
When you uninstall the I Ching application from your device, all of your data is automatically deleted by the Android system. No additional steps are required.
4
Email deletion request
If you cannot use the methods above, send an email to defnoliastudios@gmail.com. Your request will be answered within 30 days at the latest.

What Data Is Deleted?

Data Type Where It Is Stored Deletion Status
Consultation history (hexagram results) Your device only Deleted instantly
Language preference Your device only Deleted instantly
Sound / ritual settings Your device only Deleted instantly
Ad removal purchase Google Play servers Subject to Google Play policy
Advertising identifier (GAID) — AdMob Google servers Subject to Google policy
🔒 Your consultation history is never stored on our servers. For this reason, no additional server-side deletion is needed for your consultation data — only the data on your device is deleted.

For ad data processed by Google AdMob, you can use the Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete Advertising ID option on your Android device, or visit Google's ad privacy page.

Additional Retention Periods

Since Defnolia Studios does not store your consultation data on any server, no additional retention period applies. When you delete your data from your device, all consultation data is permanently destroyed and cannot be recovered.
