Purchase not visible?

Confirm that Google Play uses the same account as the original purchase, then open Defnolia Woman and use Profile → Membership options → Restore Purchases.

Delete local records

Open Profile → Data and backup → Delete all data. This removes local records, app settings, scheduled reminders and widget details.

Lost backup password?

Encrypted backups use your password and cannot be recovered by Defnolia. Keep the password separately and securely.
