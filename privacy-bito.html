1. Genel Bakış

Bu gizlilik politikası, Defnolia Studios tarafından geliştirilen Bito'nun Macerası Android uygulamasının kişisel verilerinizi nasıl işlediğini açıklar.

2. Topladığımız Veriler

Bito'nun Macerası hiçbir kişisel veri toplamaz. Uygulama yalnızca şunları cihazınızda yerel olarak saklar:

  • Tamamlanan bölümler ve kazanılan yıldızlar (oyun ilerlemesi)

Bu veriler yalnızca Android'in SharedPreferences mekanizmasıyla cihazda tutulur; hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez.

Herhangi bir bulut sunucusu, veritabanı veya uzak API kullanılmamaktadır.

1. Overview

This privacy policy describes how Defnolia Studios handles your personal data in the Bito's Adventure Android application.

🔒 Our core principle: This app collects no personal data. Progress and settings are stored only on your device. There are no ads. No data is shared with third parties.

2. Data We Collect

Bito's Adventure collects no personal data. The app only stores the following locally on your device:

  • Completed levels and earned stars (game progress)
  • Sound and language preferences (app settings)
  • Onboarding completion status

These values are stored solely via Android's SharedPreferences mechanism; they are never transmitted to any server.

3. Ads and Third-Party Services

❌ NO ads — the app is completely ad-free.
❌ NO analytics SDK (Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
❌ NO ad network (AdMob, Meta Audience Network, etc.)
❌ NO social media integration
✅ Fonts are bundled with the app (no internet required).

4. Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored exclusively on your device's local storage. Android's application sandbox prevents other apps from accessing this data. Uninstalling the app automatically deletes all data.

No cloud servers, databases, or remote APIs are used.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Bito's Adventure is designed for children aged 4–12 and complies with Google Play's Designed for Families program requirements:

  • No personal data is collected from children under 13
  • No behavioural advertising is shown
  • No mandatory in-app purchases
  • A math-based parental gate protects parent-only sections

We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable European child data protection regulations (GDPR-K).

6. Your Rights

  • Delete your data: Uninstalling the app removes all local data
  • Contact us: Use the e-mail address below for any privacy enquiries

7. Policy Changes

If this page is updated, the "Last updated" date will change. Material changes will be announced via an app update.
